Paint the standing cards before the network call (sw v26)

The "three ways to respond" and "body & mind" cards get their copy from
applyStaticLabels(), which only ran inside renderFromData() — i.e. only
on a successful /api/load. When the load failed, both cards rendered as
empty shells: no chips, no reminder line, no habit state.

Their content is entirely local (copy from I18N, habit state from
localStorage), so there was never a reason to gate it on the network.
Paint both at the top of loadApp() instead. Habits initialise as
uncommitted in that case, so toggles persist locally and reconcile on the
next successful load.
