Body & mind: drop the database side, keep the buttons (sw v27)

The meditate/move toggles were writing to two new columns on entries,
which meant a schema migration. This project had never run one, so the
tooling didn't exist and production sat broken on a 500 from the missing
columns while we sorted it out. Not worth it for a nudge.

The toggles now live entirely in the browser: state keyed by date in
localStorage, so they start fresh each morning and survive a refresh
during the day. Nothing is sent to the server.

Reverted: the meditated/moved columns and their migration, /api/habits
and its tests, the habit fields in submit/load/weekly-summary, the habit
marks in recent mornings, and the scripts/migrate.mjs helper written to
apply the migration.

Kept: the rating column in schema.sql, which the live database has had
for a while but the checked-in schema never documented.

Trade-off accepted: no habit tallies in the weekly letter, no marks in
history, and ticking a workout on the phone won't show on the laptop.

app/netlify is now byte-identical to f450003.
