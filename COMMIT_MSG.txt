Expander becomes a sticker + / – button (sw v30)

The bare ▾ chevron was too quiet to read as a control. Replaced with the
sticker treatment already used by the "you're special" game: white face,
2px ink border, 9px radius, hard pastel offset, with the glyph swapping
+ / – on state. Hover lifts it, press pushes it in.

The offset uses the ways card's own plum rather than a second accent, so
the card keeps to one accent as the style guide asks.

The warm-serif theme gets a plainer bordered square — same shape and
behaviour, none of the Cutestom sticker language.
