Centre the ways chips, put badges and word cloud on-palette (sw v28)

1. The three-ways chips are centred and roughly half again larger —
   12px label, 16px emoji, more padding.

2. Letter badges dropped the gold/rust/steel/teal gradient, which
   predated Cutestom and was the last off-band colour in the app. Each
   focus card now carries one accent (fc-0…3, cycling indigo/rose/mint/
   plum) shared by its badge and its offset shadow — one accent per card,
   and only the hero stays holographic. The accent is preserved when a
   card is selected, so the colour no longer jumps on pick; the blanket
   rose .selected shadow is gone.

3. Word cloud moved onto the four band pastels plus ink and white, with
   pill corners, a lighter 1.5px border and more padding. The panel
   itself goes 4px → 10px to match every other card.

Word-cloud text is ink rather than the house white: white on these tints
lands at 2.2–3.0:1, too thin to read at the smaller word sizes. White is
kept on the ink chip.
